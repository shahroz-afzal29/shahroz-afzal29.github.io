01 Accidental Double Orders

If a customer orders the exact same item and size twice, the system catches it immediately and asks to update the quantity instead of creating a messy duplicate entry on the bill.

02 Slow Tax Calculations

Automatically applies 16% tax for Cash or 5% tax for Card payments, plus an optional tip, removing manual calculation mistakes and speeding up checkout for customers.

03 Group Bill Splitting

Divides the final bill evenly among friends and rounds up cleanly using ceil() so groups know their exact per-person share without loose change confusion.

04 Lost Customer Feedback

Saves every customer rating, Order ID, and review with the current date and time into feedback_log.txt so the restaurant can track food quality and service after the terminal closes.

02 / AI Prompt Design

Good vs. Bad Prompting

Using clear camera angles, lighting details, and negative rules across Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Veo to get predictable results instead of random guessing.

Image Generation

Claude / GPT / Gemini
✕ Bad Prompt

"Make a picture of a futuristic city."

Result: Messy, generic neon patterns with no clear camera angle or subject.

✓ Good Prompt

"Wide angle street shot at night, wet road reflecting warm streetlights, 85mm lens, realistic shadows, photorealistic, no text."

Result: Clean camera framing, natural lighting, and controlled style.

Video Generation

Veo Video / Prompts
✕ Bad Prompt

"A video of the ocean."

Result: Shaky camera movement and random waves that don't fit a script.

✓ Good Prompt

"Slow drone shot flying low over clear blue water at sunrise, smooth forward motion, natural sunlight, no people, no text."

Result: Smooth, usable video clip that fits cleanly behind a voiceover.
